# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-16
### Cycles path tracing
- Cycles path rendering now supports the light tree (`scene.cycles.use_light_tree`, default `true`), matching per-emitter selection, MIS, thresholds, clamping, portals, and World-background sampling; disabling the tree retains flat light selection, while unsupported Light shader graphs and light/shadow linking remain reported losses.
- Path NEE and forward hits honor Cycles World, Light, Material, View Layer, and visibility settings, including holdouts, shadow catchers, caustics, film exposure/transparency, overrides, shadow-terminator offsets, and per-object ray visibility; final-render denoising reports `blender.scene.cycles.use_denoising` because no denoiser is available.
- Principled and standalone BSDF closures now follow Cycles' distinct lobe, sampling, PDF, and MIS behavior, including GGX/MULTI_GGX, anisotropy, thin film, coat, sheen, rough glass, thin walls, diffuse roughness, and Burley/random-walk subsurface; Mix/Add preserve independent closures rather than blending their parameters.
- Cycles Bump uses path ray differentials and `cycles.blur_glossy`, with Blender defaults and `FRACT`; unsupported camera/realtime footprints remain limited.
- Path hits now respect transparent and ray-bounce budgets, object ray visibility, light visibility, and Blender's closure-specific shadow transparency; `transparent_max_bounces` defaults to 8, and transparent passes no longer consume regular `max_bounces`.
- Cycles pixel sampling now uses Blender's importance-sampled film filters; `scene.cycles.pixel_filter_type` defaults to `BLACKMAN_HARRIS` and `filter_width` to 1.5, replacing Potter's prior one-pixel box for scenes without these settings.

### Volumes
- Cycles evaluates World Volume on the effective World (including View Layer overrides) and handles per-channel extinction/emission, phase mixtures, MIS, overlapping mesh/grid media, and camera-contained media.
- Continuous tracking covers World, object, and native-grid volumes, including VDB temperature and spatial shaders; eligible constant Generated grids retain deterministic voxel marching, while unsupported cases remain explicit. Blender imports report object-grid implicit-density or OBJECT-space density mismatches rather than guessing.
- `volume_bounces` is a separate Cycles setting (default 0, one scattering vertex), `volume_sampling` defaults to multiple-importance sampling, and voxel interpolation defaults to LINEAR (CUBIC is available); volume renders also support adaptive sampling with tiled VSPG guidance.
- EEVEE supports mesh-volume in-scatter, RGB voxel absorption, VDB temperature emission, and Blender's volume settings; colored realtime in-scatter and some WorldSun/probe/shadow cases remain reported limits.

### EEVEE realtime
- EEVEE Principled and standalone closures now follow captured source distributions and layer behavior, including dedicated Refraction, Translucent, Metallic, Sheen, Toon, Hair, Principled Hair, and Ray Portal previews; unsupported realtime thin-wall subsurface remains explicitly refused.
- Realtime rendering uses Blender's sample count and seed, EEVEE's Halton jitter, film accumulation/filtering, motion-blur/stereo handling, and a once-per-render World probe; the node World also supplies the camera-ray background.
- Deferred shading now preserves source G-buffer dither/precision, AdditionalInfo thickness, face-forward normals and backface IOR, Holdout alpha, and per-layer screen/probe routing; captured high-quality normals select `SNORM10` or `SNORM16`, and unrecoverable legacy values produce export losses.
- EEVEE ray tracing, Fast GI, indirect clamp, WorldSun, cubemap resolution, and `World.sun_threshold` are captured and routed into rendering; legacy settings stay unknown rather than guessed, and unavailable probe/volume/shadow behavior is reported.
- Realtime transparency now follows EEVEE's per-sample DITHERED depth test, colored Transparent closures, associated film RGB/alpha, transparent shadow passes, and material `use_transparent_shadow`; glass remains opaque to shadow rays, and unsupported backface-culling shadow behavior is reported.
- Realtime Beauty now shades flat faces and shadows per pixel, honors mesh/light visibility and camera-visible light shapes, and follows EEVEE glass, direct light, AO, and world-probe behavior.
- `render.filter_size` is now imported, exported, and configurable; realtime renders use `render.samples` instead of forcing one sample, so render cost scales with the requested count.

### Hair/Curves
- Cycles Hair Curves preserve `scene.cycles_curves.shape` and subdivisions, render RIBBONS/THICK/THICK_LINEAR (POLY uses THICK_LINEAR), and carry curve shading and shadow data; native scenes default to THICK, while legacy Blender hair with unknown settings warns and refuses rendering or strict export.
- Schema 18 imports capture per-View-Layer Cycles Hair geometry-key groups and preserve member identity, transforms, materials, visibility, curves, and dependency fingerprints; unavailable dependencies and active camera culling remain explicit limitations.
- Path hair follows Blender's raw open-key basis and strand shadows; missing Generated/UV or attribute/color interpolation is refused, while Bezier handles and NURBS knots/weights do not affect render geometry.
- Curve conversion, NURBS knots, taper, 2D fill, bevels, and curve modifiers follow Blender more closely; unsupported custom knots/conversions remain losses or refusals.
- External TrueType/OpenType fonts and VSE TEXT support Blender-compatible references, metrics, simple-LTR layout, wrapping, spacing, and fill; complex scripts and unsupported fonts/styling remain losses or refusals.

### Geometry Nodes
- Geometry Node Groups preserve interface identifiers, typed defaults, and resources through nested calls and `.blend` round trips; reachable branches evaluate lazily, recursion errors are deterministic, and pinned rotation/matrix FunctionNodes are supported.
- Geometry Nodes now support Blender-compatible topology operators/fields, typed and instance attributes, point distribution, simulation timing, and curve/hair attributes; unsupported domains and uncaptured inputs remain guarded.
- Deep connected geometry-output chains, Join Geometry, and Instance on Points use iterative dependency scheduling; unconnected inputs and unselected Switch/group branches remain lazy.
- Mesh Line offsets, Random Value defaults/hashes, POISSON ordering, creases, typed attribute propagation, and Distribute Points on Faces sampling now follow Blender; schema 14 `Mesh to Points.domain` migrates to `mode` in schema 15.
- Geometry Nodes bake settings and frame ranges round-trip, but payloads are not written and packed or unmapped bake data is reported.

### Shader nodes/textures
- Cycles and EEVEE evaluate Blender 5.2.2 shader graphs and supported Math, Mix, ramp, and texture nodes, including Wave, Magic, Brick, White Noise, Gabor, Checker, Gradient, Noise, Voronoi, Image/Environment Texture, Fresnel, Attribute, Displacement, and blackbody; unsupported modes/settings remain explicit losses or refusals.
- Image and environment sampling follows Cycles for wrap modes, boundary/negative UVs, UDIMs, projections, sRGB decode, alpha association, and texture coordinates; material texture fields and per-axis glTF samplers round-trip through shared samplers.
- Shader metadata preserves links, socket flags/order, menu IDs, and Simulation I/O identifiers; migrations record missing legacy output defaults and repair MixRGB Alpha/curve/ramp state where possible, refusing unrecoverable invalid data.

### Blender import/export & schema
- Scene JSON now migrates through schema 18: v1→2 converts to named UV layers; v2→3 adds native shading/colors/attributes; v3→4 premultiplies legacy EXRs; v4→5 converts color curves; v5→6 migrates fisheye to panorama; v6→7 adds `matrix_basis`; v7→8 moves visibility to `ray_visibility`.
- Later migrations preserve compatibility: schema 10 repairs eligible native Dynamic Paint outputs; 11 restores multi-input order; 13 restores animation outputs and 13→14 migrates pending constraints; 14→15 renames Mesh to Points `domain`; 15→16 records unavailable shader defaults and pass indices; 16→17 migrates MixRGB Alpha and curve/ramp state; schema 18 captures exact Blender settings without guessing unavailable legacy values.
- Blender import/export preserves shading/custom normals, UV/color/generic attributes, material slots, fake-user assets, exact imported `Light.use_shadow`, view-layer/render/light settings, and supported graph state; strict export refuses mismatches or unknown required values, while `--allow-lossy` reports permitted losses.
- Mesh tessellation, primitive order/UVs, Boolean/Decimate topology, subdivision, remesh, normals, and format winding now follow Blender more closely; loop triangles replace the removed `Mesh::triangulate` path, and empty/degenerate geometry works in more formats.
- `.blend` import now supports Blender-compatible F-Curves, NLA, constraints, drivers, camera/light animation, disk caches, Dynamic Paint, particle settings, and external fonts; unavailable source data and encodings are reported instead of silently defaulted.
- Solid previews now preserve Blender Workbench lighting, world background, view transforms, material display values, and documented backface-culling limits; Potter-native materials without viewport values use their own properties.

### Animation, constraints & simulation
- Action/driver animation covers cameras, lights, visibility/custom properties, transforms, shape keys, Ocean time, and shared Actions; unresolved RNA paths are ignored and unsupported animated parameters are reported.
- CLIP NLA supports scaled/reversed ranges, animated time/influence, blend modes, fades, track mute/solo/order, and component-wise channels; META/TRANSITION/SOUND, tweak mode, sampled/muted controls, and sync-length recalculation remain unsupported.
- Standard, Action, Child Of, Stretch To, Armature/B-Bone, and Follow Track constraints follow Blender target spaces, capture timing, and matrix behavior; targetless captures defer until assignment and uncaptured legacy state migrates to compatibility.
- Dynamic Paint PAINT/WEIGHT surfaces replay Blender-ordered brushes, frames, falloff, drying, dissolve, outputs, and cache ranges; unsupported surfaces and legacy brushes without recoverable `paint_source` remain warned/refused.
- Particle systems support Blender-compatible schedules, textures, distributions, rotations, paths, children, and particle-driven Instance/Explode; solver gaps remain recorded, and `rate`/`emission_rate` are refused in favor of `count` and frame range.
- Baked cloth/soft-body disk frames are content-addressed assets with Blender-style interpolation; omitted source frames are export losses, and unsupported cache encodings remain refused.

### Modifiers & mesh operations
- Blender-compatible evaluation now covers Bevel, Solidify, Mirror, Screw, Array, Shrinkwrap, Remesh, Subdivision, Decimate, Sculpt, attribute transfer, particles, and other modifiers, carrying topology, attributes, UVs, normals, and materials where supported.
- Modifier `show_viewport` and `show_render` are now separate from `enabled`; previews/inspect/apply and saved renders/bakes use their respective stacks, and `.blend` import no longer collapses a modifier hidden in only one context.
- Breaking: `modifier.apply` now applies only the selected modifier and leaves the rest of the stack intact; `modifier.update` merges `set.params` key-by-key for the same type, with `null` restoring defaults, while changing type replaces parameters.
- `modifier.reorder` now refuses moves blocked by Blender's pinned/original-data ordering instead of partially moving; Boolean defaults to Blender's EXACT solver, while MANIFOLD and unsupported FLOAT collection cases are refused.
- Dynamic topology warns `sculpt.dyntopo.face_data_lost` when corner data such as UVs cannot be preserved; applying modifiers reports dropped attributes and retains recoverable multires detail instead of silently deleting it.
- `uv.unwrap`, `mesh.triangulate`, mesh/volume conversion, and voxel remesh now use Blender-compatible algorithms; unsupported frustum resampling and curve conversions remain explicit refusals.

### CLI/workflow
- Breaking: `fisheye` is removed in favor of panorama `fisheye_equidistant` or `fisheye_equisolid`; schema 5 cameras migrate to full-turn equidistant, and equisolid uses `fisheye_lens` instead of `lens_mm`.
- Breaking: `seed` is removed from `sculpt.stroke`, `sculpt.dyntopo`, `paint.vertex`, `paint.weight`, and `paint.texture`; requests supplying it are rejected, while saved history drops it with `operation.seed_retired`.
- Breaking: `pose_lite` and `boundary_lite` sculpt brushes are removed without aliases; use `pose` or `boundary` with optional `deform_type`, and old history loads with `operation.brush_retired` but replay is refused.
- Breaking: physics uses Blender 5.2.2 RNA names/defaults and refuses Potter-only or unsupported non-default properties; `mesh.remesh` and `mesh_sequence_cache.scale` are removed, and required null references are rejected.
- `--allow-lossy` import now records accepted loss IDs on the scene; evaluation warns `accepted_loss` for them, operations still refuse newly introduced invalid data, and export does not repeat already accepted import losses.
- Generic glTF nodes without `extras.potter.id` now use deterministic sanitized names as IDs; VRM append/replace, avatar update/clear, format validation, and additional importable VRM formats are available.
- New local-material defaults match Blender where applicable, including double-sided materials, pass index `0`, Volume Absorption/Scatter color `0.8`, Sheen Roughness `0.5`, and light `soft_falloff: true`; volume/path sampling defaults are documented above, and point/spot intensity is Cycles' `energy / (4π)`.

### CI/tests
- Blender 5.2.2 oracle coverage spans rendering, volumes, shader/Geometry Nodes, modifiers, animation, import/export, and simulation; weighted CI shards retain the required `test` check.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-13
- `pot workflow refine` now requires `-p/--provider <NAME>` and `-m/--model <ID>`, sent to jcode as `<NAME>:<ID>` (examples: `openai`, `openai-api`, `claude-oauth`, `copilot`, `openrouter`) for the modeler and every reviewer; agents no longer fall back to jcode's default model.
- `pot workflow refine` counts a review as progress only when it resolves a finding for the first time and reopens none, so a run that fixes one finding per review while the reviewer finds another no longer stops as `stalled`; the reviewer gets every resolved finding and reports one that reappears as `reopened` under its original ID.
- `pot workflow refine` downscales every reference image to at most 1568 px on the longer edge and sends it as lossy WebP (quality 85), keeping large photos within provider image limits; this adds the `webp` crate (static libwebp).
- The `pot workflow refine` floating-part check now covers every visible part with evaluated bounds (curves, surfaces, text, metaballs, point clouds, volumes, collection instances), not only meshes, so a mesh resting on a curve is no longer reported as floating.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-10
- The repository is now a workspace: the engine library moved to `crates/potter-core` (crate `potter_core`, including the typed command layer `potter_core::cli::CliCommand::execute`), the root `potter` package is the `pot` binary only (argument parsing, `--json` envelopes) with the end-to-end tests in `tests/`, and shared package metadata, dependencies, and lints live in `[workspace.package]`, `[workspace.dependencies]`, and `[workspace.lints]`. Release artifacts (`potter-<target>`, `potter.rb`, `potter-installer.sh`) are unchanged; CI runs fmt/check/test/clippy with `--workspace`.
- New `pot workflow` subcommand for jcode-sdk driven agent workflows (`crates/potter-workflow`); `pot workflow -h` lists them. `pot workflow refine -i <image> [-i <image>...]` models from reference images, renders `front,back,left,right,top,iso` previews, has a fresh reviewer agent report blocking findings as schema-validated JSON, and has the modeler repair exactly those findings; output defaults to `<first image stem>-refine/` with the project in `scene/` and per-iteration renders, `review.json`, and `fix-report.json` in `iter-NN/`, and `--json` wraps the summary, or an error, in the usual `pot` response envelope. Convergence controls: explicit blocking criteria with non-blocking `non_findings` (sub-tolerance differences, shading artifacts, unseen sides), decided non-findings carried into every later review, stable finding IDs that follow-up reviews must mark `resolved` or `persists`, modeler fix reports that may mark findings `blocked`, a deterministic floating-part geometry check over visible meshes, and `converged` / `stalled` (`--stall-limit`) / `max_iterations_reached` outcomes. The modeler edits the scene only through in-process `pot_apply`/`pot_inspect`/`pot_schema`/`pot_undo` session tools. The private jcode runtime starts with `JCODE_NO_AUTO_UPDATE=1`: otherwise its empty home makes jcode download the latest release and restart mid-turn, dropping the turn and leaving the restarted server running.
