# Changelog

## Unreleased
- glTF, GLB, VRM 1.0 and VRM 0.x export convert node-graph materials (imported from `.blend` or authored with node ops) through one resolver (`exchange/material_surface.rs`) that follows Blender 5.2's glTF exporter: Principled BSDF inputs (alpha, emission, transmission, IOR, clearcoat, sheen, specular, anisotropy), image textures with UV map and Mapping (`KHR_texture_transform`), Normal Map, Separate Color and multiply-Mix packing into metallic-roughness and occlusion (also for separate metallic and roughness images), Emission and Add Shader, Background as `KHR_materials_unlit`, and alpha from `Mix(Fac, Transparent)` and clip patterns. Custom graphs export with `material.shader_graph.*` losses for parts glTF cannot express; invalid graphs are errors. Materials no node references never block export. Documented divergences from Blender are listed in `docs/spec.md` (Mix alpha, Background strength, sheen weight, Mix with emission).
- Non-VRM glTF now writes `alphaMode` and `alphaCutoff` (previously only VRM did), so blend and clip materials keep their alpha mode in GLB and glTF. `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` is written only when a channel exceeds 1, and `KHR_materials_ior` only with transmission or specular (the default Potter IOR no longer appears on every material). Textures with a transform list `KHR_texture_transform` in `extensionsRequired`.
- Samplers follow Blender: Linear uses LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR and Closest uses NEAREST_MIPMAP_NEAREST minification; wrap modes map Repeat, Clamp to edge, and Mirrored repeat.
- Textures are packed per glTF channel: any R/G/B/A channel from any image (previously only single-channel G/B masks), with derived PNGs written from raw encoded values. Separate metallic and roughness textures pack into one metallic-roughness texture instead of being refused.
- Blender-imported meshes export UVs (`blender_uv_layers`, flipped to glTF's V convention), smoothing (`blender_polygon_smooth`), and sharp edges (`blender_edge_flags`) instead of being refused; selection, cached topology and `sharp_face` are not written. Smooth-shaded `.blend` meshes previously exported flat normals silently.
- MToon v0 writes `_EmissionMap` and `_BumpScale` from the material; MToon rejects unlit, clearcoat, sheen, specular, and anisotropy surfaces with `vrm.mtoon_<name>`.
- Mesh-rebuilding modifiers carry shading (`shade_smooth`, `sharp`, `custom_normal`, `blender_polygon_smooth`, `blender_edge_flags.sharp`) with Blender 5.2.2 semantics, checked by `shading_oracle` against Blender-generated fixtures in `tests/fixtures/pr4_shading/`. Mirror averages seam custom normals per smooth fan; Array and Mirror give a merged edge its first source's flags; Subdivision sharpens non-manifold edges (left by coincident mirror faces) as OpenSubdiv does; Triangulate, Solidify, Weld (ALL and CONNECTED), Wireframe, Edge Split (`use_edge_angle`, `split_angle`), Screw, Skin, Decimate, Mask, Build, voxel Remesh and Bevel (smooth flags, strip and corner winding, `mark_sharp` and `harden_normals` for one segment on edges) follow Blender's rules. Bevel recomputes custom normals, so input custom normals there report `modifier.attribute_dropped`.
- Triangulate honours `quad_method`, `ngon_method` and `min_vertices` (defaults SHORTEST_DIAGONAL, BEAUTY and 4) with Blender's BMesh diagonal, beautify and output-order rules, and faces with fewer corners than `min_vertices` stay whole; it previously ignored all three. With `keep_custom_normals` on a mesh without custom normals, the triangles keep the pre-split corner normals, as Blender does.
- Removed the shading refusal on meshes whose modifiers change faces: glTF, GLB and VRM export, collection instances, and `modifier.apply` no longer refuse shading attributes there.
- User-visible winding fix: Screw, Skin and voxel Remesh emitted faces wound opposite to Blender, which flipped their normals and backface culling. Closed screws (a full turn, at least three steps, zero offset) now close as Blender does, using float32 tolerances, so their geometry changes where it previously stayed open. Screw also honours `use_normal_flip`, which was ignored.
- Custom normals follow Blender's setter: a corner without a custom normal takes its computed normal, and a fan whose corners disagree is cut, so the edge between them becomes sharp. A face whose corners all keep the computed normal stores no custom normal, so later deform modifiers keep moving its shading; in a face that also has custom corners, the computed corners are stored as their fan normal.
- Breaking: three settings that were ignored before now fail with `UnsupportedFeature` where Blender acts: `modifier.mirror.bisect` (Mirror `use_bisect_axis` on a mirrored axis), `modifier.bevel.mark_seam` (Bevel `mark_seam` on a mesh with seam edges), and `modifier.bevel.shading_segments` (Bevel `harden_normals`, or `mark_sharp` on sharp edges, with more than one segment or `affect` VERTICES). These settings were ignored before, so scenes that use them now fail instead of evaluating.
- Documented differences not refused: Boolean face topology does not match Blender's EXACT solver (coplanar regions are not merged), so its cut-corner normals are not verified; bevel `harden_normals` on the one-segment cube differs from Blender by 3e-4 on four corners (tolerance 2e-4), tracked for the bevel-parity PR (PR14); the decimated cube differs in topology from Blender.
- glTF, GLB and VRM exports from Potter also store `extras.potter.polygons`: each face's corners (quads, n-gons, triangles), its primitive, its smooth flag, and the sharp edges, packed into binary buffer views rather than JSON. Import restores face order, corner order, materials, UVs, `shade_smooth` and `sharp` from them, so Potter quad meshes round-trip exactly and no longer report `mesh.morph_normals` because export triangulated them; the primitives still hold triangles for other readers. A 300×300 grid (90,000 quads) stores its record in 1.98 MB instead of 5.02 MB of JSON, and its GLB is about 15% smaller. Third-party glTF keeps the triangle import and its losses. Structurally malformed records fail the import; a well-formed record that does not match its triangles, or an unknown `version`, imports triangles and reports `mesh.polygon_topology`.
- Alembic files use Blender's Y-up basis: points, normals and local transforms are stored as `(x, z, -y)` and polygon winding is reversed, so Blender imports Potter's `.abc` at the placement and facing Blender gives the same scene. Potter reads every `.abc` with this basis, so `.abc` files written before this change import rotated, and mesh cache modifiers that point at them (breaking). Re-export those files. Alembic export of a zero-scale node or a parent with zero scale no longer fails on the transform inverse; constraints on such parents still report the singular-parent error. Animated meshes write per-frame normals computed from each frame's corners. A scene that stores normals imported from `.abc` (`alembic_normals`) exports to Alembic only with `--allow-lossy`, which drops them; re-import the original `.abc` to restore them.
- Zero-scale objects and degenerate faces export without error in every exchange format. Zero-area faces and fans whose normals cancel take Blender's normals (OBJ writes zero `vn` for a singular object, PLY uses the cofactor normal matrix, glTF writes up `(0, 1, 0)` for a zero normal), and an OBJ `vn 0 0 0` now imports.
- Exchange formats carry Blender 5.2 corner normals (`shade_smooth`, `sharp`, `custom_normal`) instead of dropping them. Export writes OBJ `vn` and `f v//vn` with Blender's `s 0`/`s 1` smooth lines and Blender's default axes. Potter reads every `.obj` with these axes, so `.obj` files written before this change import rotated (breaking); USD/USDA/USDC/USDZ writes object-local `normal3f[] normals` (faceVarying) with `subdivisionScheme = "none"`; FBX writes `LayerElementNormal` (ByPolygonVertex, IndexToDirect) without smoothing; Alembic writes object-local `N` (faceVarying) and accepts shading attributes; PLY writes Blender's per-vertex normals (angle-weighted face normals, or corner sums with custom normals) once any mesh in the file is shaded, and reports `mesh.shading` when a vertex's corners disagree; STL reports `mesh.shading` for faces whose corners differ from their flat normal. Import derives `shade_smooth` and `custom_normal` for every format that carries normals, OBJ `s` groups without `vn` and FBX smoothing without normals report losses, and STL facet normals that contradict the winding report `mesh.normals`. Mirrored nodes reverse winding in OBJ, PLY, and STL so faces agree with normals. The usdc writer now stores quaternions in crate order, so Blender no longer rotates transforms written with an identity orientation by 180 degrees around X.
- Preview and render shade smooth faces (`shade_smooth`, sharp edges, `custom_normal`) with Blender 5.2 corner normals: Solid, beauty (path and realtime), normal and AO interpolate them per pixel, and true displacement follows them. Flat faces without bump or normal maps keep their beauty, normal and AO output, and path-traced ray origins now use the geometric normal as Cycles does. Solid no longer multiplies in ambient occlusion, matching Blender Workbench, so flat Solid previews change. A mesh whose corner normals cannot be built falls back to flat normals with a `shading.corner_normals_failed` warning in preview and render output; the preview cache engine is bumped to `potter-cpu-path-2`, so cached previews re-render.
- Mesh-rebuilding modifiers (mirror, array, subdivision, triangulate, solidify, weld, decimate, edge split, mask, screw, skin, and wireframe) now remap vertex groups, edge creases, edge flags, skin data, and POINT/EDGE/FACE/non-UV CORNER `blender_attributes` through input vertex / polygon provenance. Subdivision refines UV layers (below); shading attributes follow Blender's per-modifier rules (see the first entries), and other Potter attributes are copied unchanged. Mirror copies swap `.L`/`.R` group names and merged plane vertices average them; subdivision and weld interpolate vertex-group weights; glTF skin export and armature/lattice deformation read evaluated weights. Boolean carries operand-A data only. A modifier that cannot carry an attribute (such as remesh) reports `modifier.attribute_dropped` warnings on `modifier.apply`, `pot inspect`, `pot export`, and `pot preview`.
- Mesh-rebuilding modifiers carry UV layers (`blender_uv_layers`, `uv_map` with its `pinned` flags, and the same-named UV attribute in `blender_attributes`) with Blender 5.2.2 semantics. Mirror flip U/V on any axes, mirror offsets, offsets and UDIM tile reflection; array per-copy offsets and start/end cap UVs (matched by layer name); solidify rim faces; wireframe; screw (including `use_stretch_u`); boolean operand A; triangulate; weld; edge split; and mask keep their UVs with their source faces. Subdivision interpolates UVs as face-varying channels for every `uv_smooth` mode, `boundary_smooth` and `use_limit_surface`, and matches Blender at levels 1–3 on meshes without creases (levels 4–5 at the default quality differ by up to 5.5e-4, where Blender's adaptive limit approximates the surface). Bevel with UV layers matches Blender for two or more segments on cube-like three-edge corners with every edge beveled; other corners are rejected with `modifier.bevel.uv_profile`. Decimate collapse keeps the UVs of the corners it keeps; its positions still differ from Blender on some meshes. Remesh and skin drop UV layers with `modifier.attribute_dropped`.
- Creased edges (`crease_edge`) are not matched yet: subdivision does not convert crease values to Blender's sharpness, so positions and UVs along creases can differ from Blender, as they did before this change.
- Modifier output changes to match Blender 5.2.2. `subdivision_type` SIMPLE now subdivides bilinearly (it was ignored, so Catmull-Clark ran) and other values are rejected. Bevel with three or more segments on cube-like corners builds Blender's corner vertex mesh; a 2 m box with `segments: 3` is 96 vertices and 98 faces, as in Blender (was 80 and 66). Screw with a full turn and an offset opens the rings; closing needs `|angle| = 2π`, zero offset and at least three steps; screw accepts 1–2 steps. Array merged vertices take the lowest-index member's data instead of averaging. Subdivision boundary-corner limit positions no longer drift with the level.
- Subdivision with UV layers builds each level's edge topology once for all layers: a 50,176-vertex grid with one UV layer takes 3.3 s at level 2 and 25 s at level 3 (was 5.4 s and 38 s), with peak memory 1.8 GB and 3.6 GB (was 1.6 GB and 3.8 GB). The memory budget counts UV layers at about 260 bytes per output corner per layer, so the same grid at level 3 with two UV layers fails with `LimitExceeded` instead of exhausting memory.
- glTF/GLB export writes each `blender_uv_layers` layer as `TEXCOORD_n` (`uv_map` takes precedence when present) instead of rejecting the mesh; the importer still reads only `TEXCOORD_0`.
- glTF, GLB and VRM export write `NORMAL` from Blender 5.2 corner normals (`shade_smooth`, `sharp`, `custom_normal`, all-smooth vertex normals) plus morph `NORMAL` deltas, so smooth and custom shading survive export instead of being rejected; one primitive per used material slot preserves per-face materials; VRM 1.0 reverses triangle winding when it bakes a negative scale. The importer restores smooth flags and custom normals. Shading on meshes whose modifiers change faces exports with their shading, and `modifier.apply` no longer refuses it; collection instances merge every source's shading and rotate custom normals. `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` is omitted when it has no effect.
- VRM 0.x export rejects Creative Commons license names that grant more modification or commercial rights than the canonical metadata (`vrm.v0.meta_license_modification`, `vrm.v0.meta_license_commercial`); glTF/GLB/VRM export rejects skinned vertices with no deform-bone weight (`mesh.skin_unweighted_vertex`), and VRM also rejects more than four influences per vertex (`vrm.skin_influence_limit`).
- New `tests/vrm-consumer` harness and `vrm-consumer` CI job: builds a representative avatar through `pot`, validates with the Khronos glTF Validator, official VRM schemas and a per-file warning allowlist, and checks skinning, morphs, expressions, look-at, spring collisions and rendering in `@pixiv/three-vrm` 3.5.5 (patched to bind shared materials once and to select look-at range maps per spec). Supported targets and the manual VSeeFace procedure are in `docs/vrm-acceptance.md`.
- `tests/vrm-consumer` checks VRM 0.x bone look-at against the spec gaze origin and imports every fixture with the pinned VRM Add-on for Blender 4.10.2 in Blender 5.2.x (`test/blender-addon.test.mjs`); the `vrm-consumer` CI job installs Blender and fails if it is missing.
- VRM 0.x writes sideways spring `gravityDir` in the facing-corrected frame UniVRM expects, and MToon blend materials with `transparent_with_z_write` export as `_BlendMode` 3 (TransparentWithZWrite, queue 2501+offset).
- glTF import welds Potter-exported vertex copies back to their source vertices, shares vertices across primitives only when their whole payload matches, and checks morph `NORMAL`s for every file (third-party quad meshes with shape keys still report `mesh.morph_normals`, because their quads arrive as triangles). Plain glTF/GLB export now refuses a posed armature under shape keys (`rig.skin_shape_key_pose`).
- `pot import --mode append` keeps the scene's avatar settings; undo, redo and import report `changes.avatar.updated`; MToon validation errors point at `/avatar/mtoon/{material}/{field}`; `pot validate --format blend` counts the avatar loss in `format_loss`.
- Grease Pencil nodes now expose world-space `bounds` and `dimensions` including stroke thickness in `pot inspect`. The `pot workflow refine` floating-part check detects detached drawings and recognizes meshes supported by grounded strokes; empty drawings and invisible layers have no bounds.
- Added `avatar.update` / `avatar.clear` for typed native VRM avatar settings, VRM 1.0 (`vrm`) and VRM 0.x (`vrm0`) export, and `pot validate --format vrm|vrm0` preflight checks. Version-specific unrepresentable settings are rejected rather than silently dropped.
- Fixed glTF coordinate conversion for node matrices and quaternion rotations; morph POSITION accessors now include bounds, bone-parented objects round-trip under their bone nodes, and imported rotation curves use Blender's `(w,x,y,z)` component order.
- `rig.generate_basic_human` now creates a pelvis-height `hips` bone parenting the spine and legs, while retaining the root control, and pre-bends knees forward and elbows backward by `0.025 * scale` toward their IK poles so leg and arm IK can bend (Blender leaves perfectly straight chains straight); IK targets on ordinary object controls now evaluate correctly.
- Bevel with the square profile (`profile` 1) and an even segment count builds Blender's square corner mesh on three-edge cube-like corners, so two- and four-segment square bevels match Blender instead of falling back to an approximate corner. Bevel with UV layers rejects the corners its construction does not cover with `modifier.bevel.uv_profile` and a non-empty `rejected_corners` list; profile 0 (square inward) on UV layers is rejected naming `profile` as the parameter.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-13
- `pot workflow refine` now requires `-p/--provider <NAME>` and `-m/--model <ID>`, sent to jcode as `<NAME>:<ID>` (examples: `openai`, `openai-api`, `claude-oauth`, `copilot`, `openrouter`) for the modeler and every reviewer; agents no longer fall back to jcode's default model.
- `pot workflow refine` counts a review as progress only when it resolves a finding for the first time and reopens none, so a run that fixes one finding per review while the reviewer finds another no longer stops as `stalled`; the reviewer gets every resolved finding and reports one that reappears as `reopened` under its original ID.
- `pot workflow refine` downscales every reference image to at most 1568 px on the longer edge and sends it as lossy WebP (quality 85), keeping large photos within provider image limits; this adds the `webp` crate (static libwebp).
- The `pot workflow refine` floating-part check now covers every visible part with evaluated bounds (curves, surfaces, text, metaballs, point clouds, volumes, collection instances), not only meshes, so a mesh resting on a curve is no longer reported as floating.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-10
- The repository is now a workspace: the engine library moved to `crates/potter-core` (crate `potter_core`, including the typed command layer `potter_core::cli::CliCommand::execute`), the root `potter` package is the `pot` binary only (argument parsing, `--json` envelopes) with the end-to-end tests in `tests/`, and shared package metadata, dependencies, and lints live in `[workspace.package]`, `[workspace.dependencies]`, and `[workspace.lints]`. Release artifacts (`potter-<target>`, `potter.rb`, `potter-installer.sh`) are unchanged; CI runs fmt/check/test/clippy with `--workspace`.
- New `pot workflow` subcommand for jcode-sdk driven agent workflows (`crates/potter-workflow`); `pot workflow -h` lists them. `pot workflow refine -i <image> [-i <image>...]` models from reference images, renders `front,back,left,right,top,iso` previews, has a fresh reviewer agent report blocking findings as schema-validated JSON, and has the modeler repair exactly those findings; output defaults to `<first image stem>-refine/` with the project in `scene/` and per-iteration renders, `review.json`, and `fix-report.json` in `iter-NN/`, and `--json` wraps the summary, or an error, in the usual `pot` response envelope. Convergence controls: explicit blocking criteria with non-blocking `non_findings` (sub-tolerance differences, shading artifacts, unseen sides), decided non-findings carried into every later review, stable finding IDs that follow-up reviews must mark `resolved` or `persists`, modeler fix reports that may mark findings `blocked`, a deterministic floating-part geometry check over visible meshes, and `converged` / `stalled` (`--stall-limit`) / `max_iterations_reached` outcomes. The modeler edits the scene only through in-process `pot_apply`/`pot_inspect`/`pot_schema`/`pot_undo` session tools. The private jcode runtime starts with `JCODE_NO_AUTO_UPDATE=1`: otherwise its empty home makes jcode download the latest release and restart mid-turn, dropping the turn and leaving the restarted server running.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-08
- CI installs Blender 5.2.2 on Linux and runs tests with `POTTER_REQUIRE_BLENDER=1`, which turns a missing Blender into a test failure instead of a skip; all Blender-gated tests share one discovery helper. `rust-toolchain.toml` pins Rust 1.99.0 so clippy no longer depends on the runner image's Rust (1.98.0 runners flagged `clippy::float_cmp` in `tests/instances.rs`). The TARGET_PROJECT boundary regression pins Potter's hit because Blender 5.2.2 reads uninitialized boundary data there (x=0.4463836 on macOS arm64, 0.65 on Linux x86-64), and Remesh vertex parity allows 2.5e-5 for platform-dependent float32 SHARP QEF rounding.
- Relicensed potter under GPL-3.0-or-later (previously Apache-2.0) because evaluators include code derived from Blender (GPL-2.0-or-later); source files derived from Blender or OpenVDB now carry upstream attribution headers.
- Geometry Nodes and shader Math/Vector Math follow Blender's safe-math semantics: divide/modulo/snap/sqrt/log out of domain return 0, wrap with equal bounds returns min, `sign(0)` is 0, ROUND is `floor(x + 0.5)`, MODULO is `fmod`, smooth min/max use Blender's cubic curve, COMPARE uses `|a - b| <= max(epsilon, 0)` (shader default epsilon 0.001), and vector WRAP no longer collapses to 0; a Blender 5.2.2 oracle covers 144 cases.
- Mesh edits (split, subdivide, dissolve, bevel, fill, merge) carry a single-source attribute value onto new elements and recompute corner values when a face's corner count changes.
- Follow Track undistortion normalizes lens distortion with focal length, clip size, pixel aspect, and MILLIMETERS/PIXELS units, matching Blender's evaluated result.
- Follow Track validation accepts Blender's empty camera-object default and resolves track/object references by Blender name or Potter ID.
- USD export writes Blender-parseable quaternions/primvars and a valid SkelRoot joint order and preserves imported armature/deformer metadata; glTF adds joint nodes before parenting; FBX links skin clusters in the correct direction and binary FBX encodes Deformer properties, SubDeformer names, and Indexes arrays so Blender imports rigs and shape keys; Alembic allows evaluated vertex-group metadata only with lossy export.
- Binary FBX object records use the required int64 ID and two strings for every object type, fixing Blender import of scenes with materials.
- Dependencies: png 0.18, glam 0.33, sha2 0.11, lz4_flex 0.14; scene locking uses `std::fs::File::try_lock` instead of `fs4`. Tests that need Blender skip cleanly when it is not installed.
- Blender export preserves exact mesh element and loop order, custom attributes and normals, and bit-exact object world matrices; the realistic Boolean/Decimate/Solidify stack now matches at each modifier prefix, with a Blender-gated non-natural edge-order round trip regression.
- `pot render` treats an enabled but empty sequencer as a no-op, uses the scene camera, and ignores unsupported audio codecs for image output; Blender-import regressions cover default-scene rendering and strict feature errors on the realistic scene.
- Blender round trips preserve world display/background colors, area-light shape and dimensions, curve resolution and material slots, and per-view hide state independently from global visibility flags.
- Blender import stores native Surface Deform, Mesh Deform, and Laplacian Deform bind arrays in `Modifier.binding_data`. Laplacian evaluation matches Blender within 1e-5 for deformed bind states, repeated rotation solves, and hook-moved anchors; export reuses unchanged native bindings from the original compatibility `.blend`, with geometry/settings checked after reopen.
- Native Surface Deform binds now map Blender's evaluated target vertex order (including modifier stacks and shape keys) into the evaluated operand mesh, use the bind-time target-to-owner transform, and skip with typed topology warnings when target vertex or polygon counts change; Blender regressions cover subdivision, Array, transforms, and Potter binding against modified targets.
- Modifier binding evaluates only its required mesh-dependency closure; preview skips unsupported objects with typed `feature_id` warnings, while whole-scene inspect returns per-item evaluation errors and targeted inspect remains typed.
- Breaking: modifier and constraint `params` now use Blender RNA property names and uppercase enum identifiers. Renamed modifier params include Array `relative_offset`/`constant_offset` → `relative_offset_displace`/`constant_offset_displace`, Mirror `axis`/`merge` → `use_axis`/`use_mirror_merge`, Weld `distance` → `merge_threshold`, Warp `from_object`/`to_object` → `object_from`/`object_to`, Volume To Mesh `iso_level` → `threshold`, Armature `armature` → `object`, and Mesh Sequence Cache `read`/`interpolate` → `read_data`/`use_vertex_interpolation`; unknown per-type params and enum values now fail validation.
- Constraint create/update now enforce semantic RNA parameter ranges and scene references; valid Blender axis identifiers and Transformation mapping enums are accepted, and attribute-modifier errors include operation JSON pointers. Missing physics object and brush references identify their settings paths.
- Breaking: `node.create` primitive params now use Blender operator RNA names and enum values; cylinder/cone cap fill, circle fill, torus radius mode, and per-primitive operator schemas are supported, and non-geometry kinds accept empty `params` objects.
- Geometry Nodes evaluate mesh primitives with node-native sockets and semantics, separately from `node.create` operators: Cube's vector Size/XYZ vertex counts, Grid's unit-size/vertex-count defaults, UV Sphere's phase, Cylinder/Cone layouts (Cone spans Z=0 to Depth), Circle/Line geometry, Ico Sphere support, and signed/zero dimensions follow Blender's node behavior.
- Icosphere subdivision now matches Blender's single-pass multicut vertex and face ordering and float32 sphere projection at subdivision levels 1–5.
- Boolean DIFFERENCE now subtracts disconnected cutter components independently; Blender-gated strict geometry regressions cover separated, face-touching, and top-coplanar array-box cutters with manifold, component, 1e-5 surface, and 1e-6 relative-volume checks.
- Mesh-reading modifiers and geometry constraints now consume dependency-ordered evaluated operand meshes at the current frame; detected operand cycles return `EVALUATION_FAILED` instead of silently using base geometry.
- Blender export correctly recognizes linked mesh data and preserves the output path while restoring shape-key actions, allowing Alembic Mesh Cache and Transform Cache round trips to complete.
- Blender spot-light export keeps the shared math module available throughout scene restoration; a Blender-gated round trip verifies spot size and blend.
- Blender 5.2.2 Boolean surfaces match within 1e-5, but EXACT/FLOAT solver topology is not reproduced; enabled topology-dependent suffixes and applying a Boolean ahead of such a suffix now report `modifier.boolean.downstream_topology`, also listed as not supported in the feature catalog.
- Blender 5.2 F-Curve imports preserve resolved Bezier handle coordinates and invert time-axis cubic segments during sampling, fixing transform and animated Follow Path parity.
- Blender Key-datablock shape-key actions, muted keys/FCurves, relative weights, and linear interpolation now evaluate on mesh data; Surface Deform operands consume the evaluated target, with Blender-gated 1e-5 target and bound-mesh parity coverage.
- Blender bevel profiles use superellipse chord spacing and face-space offsets; four-or-more-edge vertices with multiple beveled edges now report `modifier.bevel.high_valence_vertex_mesh` instead of approximate geometry, cataloged unsupported and covered by the gear-stack prefix regression.
- Exact Boolean BSP traversal uses explicit worklists instead of recursive calls, preventing stack overflow on tilted cylinder cutters; regression coverage verifies the resulting box bore remains closed and removes volume.
- Armature modifier skinning now converts between mesh and armature object spaces before applying bone matrices, preserving weighted deformations when rig and mesh share a world offset.
- Blender 5.2.2 modifier parity now covers Simple Deform's axis/limit formulas, zero-thickness Solidify's duplicated shell, curve bevel tessellation, and order-sensitive Array/Mirror stacks.
- Blender curve round trips retain `Curve.twist_mode` and match minimum-twist beveled `to_mesh` point sets; Clamp To and Spline IK preserve Blender's disabled CUSTOM-space results, and Shrinkwrap TARGET_PROJECT now uses interpolated vertex-normal surfaces.
- Legacy Blender IK now applies Blender's square-root-normalized weights to combined position/orientation tasks; supported elbow and non-ellipse swing chains retain strict 1e-5 parity, while two-axis X/Z Swing ellipse limits report `constraint.ik.swing_ellipse_limit` as unsupported. Shrinkwrap TARGET_PROJECT continues to match the interpolated vertex-normal surface.

- Blender modifier round trips preserve bound Surface/Mesh/Laplacian Deform behavior after moving bound geometry; PC2/MDD/Alembic cache settings normalize Blender RNA arrays and match after reopening.
- Boolean modifier evaluation now dissolves adjacent coplanar fragments; round-trip coverage checks DIFFERENCE/UNION/INTERSECT with object/collection operands and EXACT/FLOAT solvers using closed-surface distance, volume, manifold, and component invariants. `pot inspect` reports evaluated mesh faces as vertex-index lists alongside positions.
- Restored the supported polygon-mask feature catalog entry for mask create, update, and delete operations; Bezier spline handles remain unsupported.
- Blender-gated COLLAPSE now preserves Blender's retained BMesh face normals when triangulating and matches strict topology/1e-5 position parity for the first-collapse boundary probe, triangulation, X symmetry, vertex groups, vertex-group factor 0.5, and a ratio-0.5 open grid. Inverted vertex-group COLLAPSE remains cataloged unsupported at every ratio below 1.0 after its ratio-0.7 probe diverged by one face/edge and 1.554746e-1 in vertex position.
- Decimate UNSUBDIV and DISSOLVE now follow Blender's `bmesh_decimate_unsubdivide` / `bmesh_decimate_dissolve` algorithms (BLI heap tie order, float32 angle/normal costs, delimit rules, degenerate-ear checks, edge-pair collapse order); Blender-gated fixtures assert identical topology and 1e-5 per-vertex parity for UNSUBDIV iterations 1–2 and DISSOLVE angle, NORMAL, MATERIAL, SEAM, SHARP, UV, and boundary settings (0.0 maximum vertex error).
